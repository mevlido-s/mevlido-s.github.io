for bells, violin, accordion and Live Electronics. 

This piece is inspired by the poem "Earth Gods" by Khalil Gibral. 

"*We are but gods holding a world and held by it,*
*Fates that sound the trumpets*
*Whilst the breath and the music come from beyond.*
*And I rebel.*
*I would exhaust myself to emptiness.*
*I would dissolve myself afar from your vision*"

The three instruments are isoleted by walls, rooms, and other things that are meant to let them be distant. 

Bells as memories. 
Violin as a mormour. 
Accordion as a far away being. 

And then something.
