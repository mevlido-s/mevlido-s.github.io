Gentle Fire Basel is a rework made with Ivan Liuzzo initiated in 2021, based on
Alvin Lucier’s composition Gentle Fire (1971).
The piece consists in transforming a sound
image, present in a list, into another sound image,
part of a second list.

This process should be slow and clear.
Some sound ‘images’ written in the lists are sound
events from the environment, others are speculative and imaginative.

*[...] For me those lists were ways of making beautiful scores. I thought of them as poetry. [...]*
*‘Some of the lists remind me of dreams...’*

(Chambers, Alvin Lucier. Simon Douglas)

Our rework focus within the spaces of sound sources, or the “images,” evaluated in its real, physical context - considering its relationship and interaction with the environment and any unforeseen elements, including its characteristics and the
enrichment or impoverishment derived from interacting with a “lit” or “dimmed” environment.

The sound source (or the sound image) is not treated as a pure and ideal sound object but is situated within a polyphonic, heterophonic “choral” system, where it can be the most prominent, marginal, or momentary in part.
The microphone became a means of transforma-
tion - or at least a point of transition - between the sound images, while serving as an aesthetic tool that shapes and interacts with the very concept of the image and its surroundings.

see also the [artist book](GFBook/) dedicated to this project
